The bar is measured on a versioned golden set with inputs sampled at random from real conversations in Snowflake, so the team cannot cherry-pick the test. Production monitoring on live traffic is the ungameable backstop. If quality drifts after launch, the badge is pulled automatically.

How a feature earns and keeps the badge

Two flows. Build-time is how a surface team earns the mark. Run-time is what the platform does on every live request, including pulling the badge when a feature drifts below the bar.

Drift below bar → revoke
This feature loses its badge, the others keep theirs

A revocation is the mark working, the same way a restaurant losing its A-grade is the grading system working. One badged failure does not taint the whole brand, because the badge is per feature and the firewall is automatic. Revocation rate is a leading indicator we watch on the health of the whole strategy.
